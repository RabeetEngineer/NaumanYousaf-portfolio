About the Officer

A Career Built on Fiscal Stewardship

Nauman Yousaf is a distinguished officer of the Pakistan Administrative Service (PAS), having commenced his public service through the 36th Common Training Programme. With a career anchored in the Government of Punjab's Finance Department, he has become one of the province's foremost authorities on public financial management and fiscal governance.

Currently serving as Special Secretary (Budget) in the Finance Department, Government of the Punjab, he sits at the nexus of provincial fiscal planning — overseeing the formulation, presentation, and execution of the annual Punjab budget cycle and ensuring rigorous expenditure discipline across all line departments.

Earlier, as Ex-Chairperson of the Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA), he spearheaded transformative revenue policy reforms and digital modernisation of services tax administration. As CEO of Punjab Financial Advisory Services (PFAS) and Ex-Officio Board Member of Punjab Thermal Power Limited (PTPL), he extended his governance expertise across financial strategy and the energy sector.
